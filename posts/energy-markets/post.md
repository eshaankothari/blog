Electricity is a market that has to clear every few minutes — supply has to match demand on the grid at every moment. These chapters build that market up from the simplest version: a few generators on one wire, then hundreds, then every generator in Texas.

## Chapters

- [Chapter 1 — Economic dispatch on a single bus](/blog/energy-markets/chapter-1.html): three generators, one bus, one demand. Submit your own bids, move demand, and watch the clearing price change.
- [Chapter 2 — A simulated electricity market](/blog/energy-markets/chapter-2.html): from a synthetic fleet to the real offers of 844 ERCOT generators at 5 pm on 13 September 2026, with the supply curve and a map of where the power comes from.

More chapters are on the way — transmission and congestion next.
