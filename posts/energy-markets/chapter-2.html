A Simulated Electricity Market
Simple Merit-Order Dispatch

Economics 256

Abstract

We draw a synthetic fleet of generators across six technologies. Each generator bids a quantity at price , with sizes and prices drawn from distributions typical of its technology. The market clears by solving (1) for demand : bids are stacked in merit order, and the last bid needed sets the clearing price . Switching the fleet to ERCOT replaces the draw with the real offers of every online generator in Texas during one five-minute interval.
